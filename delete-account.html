Delete Your Account
Last Updated: August 1, 2026
You can permanently delete your MysticLand account at any time — directly from within the app or by contacting our support team. This page explains exactly how to delete your account and what happens to your data when you do.

1 How to Delete Your Account

The easiest way to delete your account is directly inside the MysticLand app:

📍 Navigate to Account Deletion

Open MysticLand App → 😈 Profile (bottom right) → ⚙️ Settings → 🗑️ Delete Account Permanently
1
Open the MysticLand app on your Android phone and sign in to your account.
2
Tap the 😈 Profile icon in the bottom right corner of the navigation bar.
3
Tap the ⚙️ Settings icon in the top left corner of your profile screen.
4
Scroll down to the Danger Zone section (highlighted in red).
5
Tap Delete Account Permanently.
6
A confirmation dialog will appear. Read the warning carefully and tap Yes, Delete My Account to confirm.
7
Your account deletion request is submitted immediately. All your data will be permanently deleted within 30 days.

⛔ This action is permanent and cannot be undone. Once your account is deleted, you will not be able to recover your profile, photos, matches, or chat history. You will need to create a new account if you wish to use MysticLand again.

3 What Happens to Your Subscription

If you have an active premium subscription when you delete your account:

1
Your MysticLand account and all data will be deleted as described above.
2
Your subscription will not be automatically cancelled — you must cancel it separately through Google Play Store to stop future charges.
3
To cancel your subscription: Open Google Play Store → Subscriptions → MysticLand → Cancel Subscription.

⚠️ Important: Deleting your MysticLand account does NOT automatically cancel your Google Play subscription. Please cancel your subscription separately to avoid future charges.

4 Can't Access the App?

If you are unable to access the MysticLand app to delete your account (for example, if you have lost access to your Google account), you can request account deletion by emailing us directly:

📧 Email us at: mysticland.app@gmail.com


Please include in your email:


• Subject: Account Deletion Request
• The Google email address linked to your MysticLand account
• Your MysticLand anonymous username (if you remember it)
• A brief reason for the deletion request (optional)


We will process your request and confirm deletion within 7 business days.

5 Your Rights Under Indian Law

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act), you have the right to:

✓
Erasure: Request permanent deletion of all your personal data from MysticLand's systems.
✓
Access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you before deletion.
✓
Grievance Redressal: Contact us at any time to address privacy concerns.

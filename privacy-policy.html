2 Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide

  • Google Account: When you sign in with Google, we receive your Google email address and a unique Google ID. We do not receive your Google password.
  • Profile Information: Gender, date of birth, height, city/location preference, and anonymous username you create.
  • Turn-Ons & Preferences: Your selected preferences and desire statements you choose to share.
  • Photos: Profile photos and private photos you voluntarily upload to the app.
  • Desire Posts: Text content you write and post as your desire statement.
  • Chat Messages: Messages you send and receive within the app.

2.2 Face Verification Data

🛡️ Important: MysticLand requires a one-time face verification selfie to confirm you are a real person. This selfie is sent to Google's Gemini AI API for analysis only. Your selfie is NOT stored on our servers, NOT shared with other users, and NOT used for any purpose other than the one-time verification check. Only the verification result (pass/fail) is saved.

2.3 Location Information

  • We collect your approximate city-level location to show you nearby profiles.
  • We do NOT collect your precise GPS coordinates.
  • Location data is used only for matching and is never sold to third parties.

2.4 Payment Information

  • Subscription payments are processed securely through the Google Play Store billing system.
  • We do NOT store your credit card, debit card, or bank account details.
  • We only receive a payment confirmation from Google Play after a successful transaction.

2.5 Automatically Collected Information

  • Device type and operating system version
  • App version
  • Crash reports and error logs (for improving the app)
  • Approximate usage data (time spent in app, features used)

3 How We Use Your Information

We use your information only for the following purposes:

  • To create and manage your anonymous profile
  • To show you nearby profiles based on your city and preferences
  • To enable matching, liking, and messaging features
  • To verify your identity and prevent fake profiles
  • To process subscription payments via Google Play Store billing
  • To send push notifications about new likes, matches, and messages (if enabled)
  • To enforce our Community Guidelines and Terms of Service
  • To improve app performance and fix bugs
  • To comply with applicable Indian laws and regulations

We do not use your data for advertising or sell your data to any third party.

4 Anonymity & Your Identity

MysticLand is built on the principle of anonymous connections:

  • Your real name is never shown to other users
  • Your Google email is never visible to other users
  • You are identified only by your chosen anonymous username
  • Your face verification selfie is never shown to anyone
  • You control what photos you share and with whom
  • Photos shared in chat are automatically destroyed after viewing (for Premium users)
  • Screenshots of chats are blocked within the app

5 Third-Party Services

MysticLand uses the following trusted third-party services:

5.1 Firebase (Google LLC)

We use Firebase for user authentication, database storage, and cloud functions. Firebase is governed by Google's Privacy Policy. Data stored in Firebase is located on Google's secure servers.

5.2 Google Play Store Billing

All subscription payments are processed securely through Google Play Store billing. We never see or store your credit card, debit card, or UPI details. Payments are governed by Google Pay's Terms of Service. Supported payment methods include UPI, PhonePe, Google Pay, BHIM, and debit/credit cards.

5.3 Google Gemini AI

Face verification selfies are sent to Google's Gemini Vision API for one-time analysis. Images are processed according to Google's AI usage policies and are not retained by Google for training purposes beyond their stated data retention policies.

6 Data Storage & Security

  • All data is stored on Firebase servers (Google Cloud), which are encrypted at rest and in transit using industry-standard SSL/TLS encryption.
  • Access to user data is strictly limited to essential app functions.
  • We implement Firebase Security Rules to ensure users can only access their own data.
  • Payment data is secured by Google Play Store's PCI-DSS compliant infrastructure.
  • We regularly review our security practices to protect your data.

7 Data Retention

  • Account data: Retained as long as your account is active.
  • Chat messages: Retained until you or your match deletes them.
  • Photos: Retained until you delete them from your profile.
  • Verification selfie: Never stored — deleted immediately after the AI check.
  • Deleted accounts: All data is permanently deleted within 30 days of account deletion.
  • Payment records: Retained for 7 years as required by Indian tax law.

8 Your Rights Under DPDP Act 2023

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you have the following rights:

  • Right to Access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to Correction: Request correction of inaccurate personal data.
  • Right to Erasure: Request deletion of your personal data (account deletion).
  • Right to Grievance Redressal: Contact us to address any privacy concerns.
  • Right to Nominate: Nominate another person to exercise your rights in case of death or incapacity.

To exercise any of these rights, email us at mysticland.app@gmail.com.
